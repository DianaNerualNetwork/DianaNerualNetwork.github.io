Biography

Hi! I’m a first-year Ph.D. student at the National University of Singapore (NUS), supervised by Prof. Xiaohan Xing. I received my M.S. from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) and my B.S. from Wuhan University of Technology. During my master’s studies at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), I was supervised by Prof. Wenjian Qin.

Feel free to contact me by email if you are interested in discussing or collaborating with me.

Research Directions

My research interests lie at the intersection of Computer Vision and Machine Learning. Since 2022, I have been working on digital pathology and medical imaging, with a focus on:

  • 3D Histopathology Reconstruction. Reconstructing three-dimensional tissue structures from histopathology images.
  • Virtual Staining. Developing virtual staining methods for digital pathology.
  • Medical Image Analysis. Designing methods for medical imaging and downstream analysis tasks.
